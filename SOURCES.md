## Ocean exploration — carousel

[NOAA: How much of the ocean has been explored?](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/explored/), updated April 20, 2026. As of April 2026, 28.7% of the global seafloor was mapped at modern high resolution. Less than 0.001% of the deep seafloor had been directly observed. These are different measures.

[Original ROV photograph hosted by USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/noaa-ocean-explorations-remotely-operated-vehicle-deep-discoverer). Public domain. Credit: NOAA Ocean Exploration, 2021 North Atlantic Stepping Stones: New England and Corner Rise Seamounts.

## DART — thumbnail 1

[NASA: DART's final images prior to impact](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/darts-final-images-prior-to-impact/). Image: NASA/Johns Hopkins APL.

[NASA: Asteroid's orbit and shape changed after DART impact](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/jpl/nasa-study-asteroids-orbit-shape-changed-after-dart-impact/), March 19, 2024. Dimorphos's orbital period around Didymos was shortened by approximately 33 minutes. The thumbnail's curve is decorative, not a measured orbit.
