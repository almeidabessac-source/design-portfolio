# Christian Bessa — Design Portfolio

English portfolio for social posts, carousels and YouTube thumbnails, with After / Before comparisons.

- 3 posts, 1080 × 1350 PNG
- 1 carousel, 5 pages, 1080 × 1350 PNG
- 8 thumbnail pairs: 6 fictional-person concept studies + the 2 original science studies, 1280 × 720 PNG
- Responsive static site with full-size artwork, slide navigation, Discord copy and Reddit contact.

Publish with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root folder. No build command or external dependencies are needed.

The science studies use official source images in their Before panels. The six fictional-person studies use hypothetical source portraits. Final artwork was produced with AI assistance and is a creative interpretation; it is not scientific imagery. Source credits and article links are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). No affiliation or endorsement by NASA, NOAA or USGS is implied.

Source facts were checked November 15, 2026. The NOAA mapping figure is explicitly dated April 2026. Original agency imagery retains its source terms; no blanket license is claimed over third-party materials.


Thumbnail buttons 1–8 select the After and Before images together. Buttons 1–6 use six different fictional models generated with AI. Their before images and financial / watch-time figures are hypothetical design materials, not client results. Buttons 7 and 8 preserve the original science thumbnails. Updated November 15, 2026.
